岗位职责 / Responsibilities

  • 接待客户，介绍展厅产品与整装方案Greet customers & present showroom products.

任职要求 / Requirements

  • 两年以上销售或零售行业经验2+ years’ experience in sales or retail.
  • 中英文流利，会马来语者优先Fluent in Chinese & English; BM a plus.
  • 有建材或家居行业经验者优先Building materials / home furnishing experience preferred.
