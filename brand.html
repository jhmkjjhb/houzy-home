Pricing Communication · 价格沟通系统
价格不是促销工具，是信任工具
Pricing is not only a sales tool. It is a trust tool.
Must Show / 必须展示
基础价格、适用面积、包含项目、排除项目、最终核算条件。
Base price, applicable area, included items, excluded items, and final quotation conditions.
Must Say / 必须说明
最终报价根据户型、面积、材料等级、现场条件和安装要求确认。
Final quote depends on layout, area, material grade, site condition, and installation requirement.
Avoid / 避免
避免“全城最低”“今天不买就没了”“豪华爆款”等低信任促销话术。
Avoid low-trust discount language such as “lowest price” or “today only”.
Standard Sentence / 标准说明
价格公开，配置清楚。最终报价将根据户型、面积、材料与现场条件，由顾问核算确认。
Transparent pricing with clear specifications. Final quotation depends on layout, area, materials, and site conditions, confirmed by a consultant.

Content Field Standard · 内容字段标准
可落地的关键，是每张物料该写什么都固定
Execution works when every material has fixed content fields.
标价牌必须包含：产品区、产品名、型号、尺寸、价格、单位、包含项目、排除项目、QR。
Price cards must include zone, product name, code, size, price, unit, inclusions, exclusions, and QR.
分区牌必须包含：中文区名、英文区名、三词利益点、咨询引导。
Zone signs must include Chinese name, English name, three benefit words, and consultation prompt.
名片必须包含：姓名、职位、语言能力、WhatsApp、邮箱、门店地址。
Business cards must include name, role, languages, WhatsApp, email, and showroom address.
报价单必须包含：项目范围、材料等级、施工边界、付款节点、质保说明。
Proposals must include scope, material grade, construction boundary, payment milestones, and warranty.
员工工牌必须包含：姓名、岗位、可服务语言、个人 WhatsApp QR。
Staff badges must include name, role, service languages, and personal WhatsApp QR.
售后卡必须包含：质保范围、响应时间、报修方式、Google Review 引导。
Aftercare cards must include warranty scope, response time, support channel, and Google Review prompt.
Rule / 规则
任何物料如果无法回答“这是什么、多少钱、包含什么、下一步找谁”，就不允许进入门店使用。
If a material cannot answer what it is, how much it costs, what is included, and who to contact next, it should not enter the showroom.

Quality Control · 落地验收标准
不是做出来就算完成，要能被验收
Execution is not finished until it can be checked and approved.
Visual / 视觉

颜色是否接近标准色；logo 是否变形；留白是否足够；字距是否被压缩。
Check colour, distortion, clear space, and letter spacing.

Reading / 阅读

客户站在 1m / 3m / 8m 处是否能读清对应信息。
Check readability from 1m, 3m, and 8m.

Material / 材质

纸张、亚克力、金属字、贴膜是否符合使用场景和耐久度。
Check paper, acrylic, metal letters, films, and durability.

Sales / 销售

物料是否能帮助顾问解释价格、配置、流程和下一步行动。
Check whether materials help explain price, specification, process, and next action.

Maintenance / 维护

价格、套餐、顾问信息是否可以低成本替换，不必整套重做。
Check whether pricing, packages, and staff info can be replaced cheaply.

Consistency / 一致

官网、门店、社媒、报价单是否使用同一套 logo、色彩和话术。
Check consistency across website, showroom, social media, and proposals.
